Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our professional cleaning services and procedures.

Do I need to supply cleaning products or equipment?

No. Our trained cleaners arrive fully equipped with professional-grade, hospital-safe, and eco-friendly supplies, vacuum cleaners, and microfiber materials.

Are all cleaners background-verified and insured?

Yes, 100%. Every SparkleHome cleaner undergoes identity checks, rigorous background verification, and over 40 hours of practical cleaning and safety training.

What if I am not satisfied with the cleaning outcome?

We stand by our 100% Sparkle Guarantee. If any designated area falls short of your expectations, let us know within 24 hours and we will re-clean it free of charge.

Can I reschedule or cancel my service booking?

You can reschedule or cancel anytime up to 4 hours prior to your scheduled slot without any cancellation fee or penalty.
